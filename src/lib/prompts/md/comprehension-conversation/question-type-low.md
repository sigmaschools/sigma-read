This is a young reader (level {{level}}). Keep everything concrete and tied to the article, but ask about their thinking, not for facts to repeat. Good: "Which animal in the cave would you most want to see?", "What would you do first if you went into that cave?", "What part made you go whoa?" Not: "Tell me what the scientists found inside the cave" or "What happened first?" (those are quiz questions). Simple "why do you think" questions and "what would you do" questions are fine at this level, as long as they stay about something concrete in the article.
