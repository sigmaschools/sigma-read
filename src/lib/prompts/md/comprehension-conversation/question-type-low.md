This is a young reader (level {{level}}). Keep everything concrete and tied to the article, but prompt their thinking, not facts to repeat. Good: "Pick the animal in the cave you'd most want to see and tell me why", "Tell me the first thing you'd do if you went into that cave", "Tell me the part that made you go whoa." Not: "Tell me what the scientists found inside the cave" or "What happened first?" (those are quiz prompts). Simple "tell me why you think" and "tell me what you'd do" directives are fine at this level, as long as they stay about something concrete in the article.
