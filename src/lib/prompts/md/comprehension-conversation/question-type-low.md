- This is a young reader (level 1-2). Keep questions simple and concrete, but conversational — not quizzy. Instead of "What did the scientists find?" try "The scientists found something pretty wild in that cave — what was it?" Frame questions like you're genuinely curious what they think, not testing them. For Step 1: start with something from the article that a kid would find cool or weird. For Step 2: a simple "why" or "how" that connects two things from the text. For Step 3: a fun "what if" or personal connection.
